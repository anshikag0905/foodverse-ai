# Data Attribution & Open Source Licenses

FoodVerse AI leverages openly licensed food composition datasets, nutritional registries, and knowledge repositories to provide accessible, global food intelligence. We gratefully acknowledge the creators and contributors of the following datasets:

- **Source**: [Open Food Facts Database](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/)
- **License**: Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0

- **Terms & Usage**:

## 2. USDA FoodData Central
- **Source**: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service (FDC)
- **Sub-datasets**: Foundation Foods, SR Legacy, and Food and Nutrient Database for Dietary Studies (FNDDS).
- **License**: U.S. Public Domain (CC0 Equivalent / Open Government Data)
- **Reference**: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central, 2019. [fdc.nal.usda.gov](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov).

## 3. Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT) & Regional Seeds
- **Source**: ICMR – National Institute of Nutrition (NIN), Hyderabad, India.
- **Reference**: Indian Food Composition Tables (2017), T. Longvah, R. Ananthan, K. Bhaskarachary, K. Venkaiah.
- **Usage**: Nutritional values for staple Indian grains, pulses, spices, vegetables, and traditional recipes curated for public health education.
- **License**: Public educational reference and fair-use attribution for non-commercial open intelligence.

- **Sources**:
  - World Health Organization (WHO) Food Safety Guidelines.
